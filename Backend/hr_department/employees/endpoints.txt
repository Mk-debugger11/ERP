----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. DEPARTMENTS ENDPOINTS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
GET    /api/hr/departments/          List all departments
POST   /api/hr/departments/          Create a new department
GET    /api/hr/departments/{id}/     Retrieve department details
PUT    /api/hr/departments/{id}/     Update entire department
PATCH  /api/hr/departments/{id}/     Partial update department
DELETE /api/hr/departments/{id}/     Delete a department
