# Core HR Modules for an Enterprise ERP
## 1. Employee Management
The central module that stores and manages all employee information.

**Purpose**
* Employee Profile
* Department
* Designation
* Manager
* Contact Details
* Employment Status

---

## 2. Recruitment (Talent Acquisition)

Manages the complete hiring process.

**Purpose**

* Job Openings
* Candidates
* Interviews
* Offer Letters
* Hiring Status

---

## 3. Onboarding

Handles everything required when a new employee joins.

**Purpose**

* Document Verification
* Employee ID Creation
* Email & Account Creation
* Laptop Assignment
* Orientation

---

## 4. Attendance Management

Tracks employee attendance and working hours.

**Purpose**

* Check In / Check Out
* Working Hours
* Overtime
* Attendance Reports

---

## 5. Leave Management

Manages employee leave requests and approvals.

**Purpose**

* Leave Application
* Leave Approval
* Leave Balance
* Holiday Calendar

---

## 6. Payroll Management

Calculates and processes employee salaries.

**Purpose**

* Salary Processing
* Tax Calculation
* Bonuses
* Deductions
* Payslips

---

## 7. Performance Management

Evaluates employee performance.

**Purpose**

* Goals
* Performance Reviews
* Ratings
* Promotions
* Salary Increments

---

## 8. Learning & Development (Training)

Tracks employee learning and skill development.

**Purpose**

* Training Programs
* Certifications
* Skill Development
* Learning Progress

---

## 9. Document Management

Stores employee-related documents securely.

**Purpose**

* Resume
* Offer Letter
* Educational Certificates
* Identity Documents
* Salary Slips

---

## 10. Exit Management

Handles the employee resignation process.

**Purpose**

* Resignation
* Notice Period
* Asset Return
* Final Settlement
* Experience Letter

---

# HR Module Flow

Recruitment
↓
Onboarding
↓
Employee Management
↓
Attendance
↓
Leave Management
↓
Payroll
↓
Performance Management
↓
Training
↓
Exit Management

Throughout the employee lifecycle, the Document Management module stores all important employee records.
